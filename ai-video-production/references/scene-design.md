# Scene Design

## Inputs

Use the approved storyboard, approved character references, and the script's location, time, mood, style, and interaction needs. Define a unique location asset for each meaningful location plus time-of-day/weather/state variation.

## Required outputs

Create `05_scenes/scene_bible.xlsx` and, for each `LOCNNN` asset:

```text
LOC001_name/
  brief.md
  scene_spec.json
  candidates/
  approved/
    empty_plate_wide.png
    empty_plate_medium.png
    empty_plate_close.png
    blocking_map.png
    character_zones.json
    camera_coverage.json
    continuity_notes.md
```

The empty plates must contain no people, silhouettes, human reflections, or incidental crowd figures. `blocking_map.png` is an internal overlay that marks standing/sitting zones, entrances, exits, paths, interaction anchors, eyeline axes, and usable camera areas. The base scene plate itself stays human-free.

## Quality gate

The approved scene matches the script's mood, time, and visual world; preserves room or landscape geometry across planned coverage; accommodates character scale, paths, and prop interaction; supports required wide/medium/close framing; records lighting and color state; and preserves the intended 180-degree line where dialogue coverage requires it.
